Academic References
===================

The Digital Annealer implementation in this repository is based on the papers.

- Aramon, M., Rosenberg, G., Valiante, E., Miyazawa, T., Tamura, H., &
  Katzgraber, H. G. (2019). *Physics-inspired optimization for quadratic
  unconstrained problems using a digital annealer*. Frontiers in Physics, 7, 48.
  DOI: `<https://doi.org/10.3389/fphy.2019.00048>`_
- Tsukamoto, S., Takatsu, M., Matsubara, S., & Tamura, H. (2017). *An
  accelerator architecture for combinatorial optimization problems*. Fujitsu
  Scientific & Technical Journal, 53(5), 8-13.
- Earl, D. J., & Deem, M. W. (2005). *Parallel tempering: Theory, applications,
  and new perspectives*. Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics, 7(23), 3910-3916.
  DOI: `<https://doi.org/10.1039/B509983H>`_
- Hukushima, K., & Nemoto, K. (1996). *Exchange Monte Carlo method and
  application to spin glass simulations*. Journal of the Physical Society of
  Japan, 65(6), 1604-1608. DOI: `<https://doi.org/10.1143/JPSJ.65.1604>`_
